EFFROSYNI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

EFFROSYNI is a privacy-first, local-first application designed to help appointment-based professionals understand their workload, schedule, expected revenue, personal work patterns, and optional end-of-day Energy check-ins.

This Privacy Policy explains how EFFROSYNI handles information when you use the application.

1. Developer

EFFROSYNI is provided by:

SirrisGuides

Privacy and support contact:
sirrisguides@gmail.com

2. Privacy-First Design

EFFROSYNI is designed so that your personal work data remains on your own device.

EFFROSYNI does not require an account.

EFFROSYNI does not require you to sign in.

EFFROSYNI does not use a cloud backend to store your app data.

EFFROSYNI does not send your workload, appointment, revenue, Energy, pattern, or settings data to the developer.

3. Information EFFROSYNI Does Not Collect

EFFROSYNI does not collect or transmit your personal app data to the developer or to third parties.

In particular, EFFROSYNI does not collect:

- names of clients or patients
- client or patient initials
- telephone numbers
- email addresses
- diagnoses
- clinical notes
- medical records
- reasons for visits
- patient or client identifiers
- payment card information
- bank account information
- precise location
- advertising identifiers
- analytics data
- behavioral tracking data
- contacts
- photos
- microphone recordings
- camera recordings

EFFROSYNI is not designed to store client-identifying or patient-identifying information.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

EFFROSYNI may store the information you choose to enter into the application, including:

- appointment types
- appointment dates and times
- appointment duration
- appointment status
- expected fees
- workload preferences
- expected revenue targets
- optional end-of-day Energy check-ins
- work pattern history
- personal range information
- experiments and What If scenarios
- application settings
- privacy settings
- app lock settings

This information is stored locally on your device.

5. No Client or Patient Records

EFFROSYNI is not a CRM, electronic health record, patient record, medical record, or clinical documentation system.

You should not enter client names, patient names, diagnoses, clinical notes, medical information, reasons for visits, or other identifying information into EFFROSYNI.

EFFROSYNI is designed to work without this information.

6. Local Database and Encryption

On supported Android installations, EFFROSYNI stores application data in a local encrypted database.

The database encryption key is protected using Android platform security mechanisms.

EFFROSYNI does not transmit the database encryption key to the developer.

7. App Lock

EFFROSYNI may allow you to protect access to the application using an App Lock PIN.

The PIN itself is not stored as plain text.

App Lock is an application access control feature. It is not intended to replace the security features of your device.

8. Optional Energy Check-In

EFFROSYNI may allow you to record an optional end-of-day Energy value from 1 to 10.

This value is a personal self-recorded estimate.

It is not a medical measurement.

It is not a diagnosis.

It is not a health score.

EFFROSYNI does not interpret Energy values as evidence of a medical or psychological condition.

9. Expected Revenue

EFFROSYNI may calculate expected revenue using appointment information and expected fees entered by you.

Expected revenue is not the same as money actually received.

EFFROSYNI does not track bank transactions.

EFFROSYNI does not confirm whether a payment was made.

EFFROSYNI is not accounting software, financial advice, or a payment processing service.

10. Patterns and Personal Range

EFFROSYNI may display patterns and associations based on your recorded work history.

These patterns are descriptive.

They do not establish causation.

They do not diagnose burnout, fatigue, illness, or any other health condition.

Personal Range is based on your own recorded history.

It is not a medical recommendation.

It is not a safe-work limit.

It is not professional health advice.

11. Backup, Export, and Restore

EFFROSYNI may allow you to export backups or other files.

These actions occur only when you choose to perform them.

When you export a file, the operating system allows you to choose where that file is saved.

Depending on your choice, the file may be stored on your device or in a storage location or service selected by you.

EFFROSYNI does not automatically upload your backup or export files to the developer.

You are responsible for protecting any exported files and for choosing where they are stored.

12. Data Sharing

EFFROSYNI does not sell your personal data.

EFFROSYNI does not share your personal app data with advertisers.

EFFROSYNI does not share your workload data with third parties.

EFFROSYNI does not use your app data for advertising or profiling.

13. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

EFFROSYNI does not include advertising.

EFFROSYNI does not use advertising SDKs.

EFFROSYNI does not use analytics SDKs to monitor how you use the application.

EFFROSYNI does not use third-party tracking SDKs to create advertising or behavioral profiles.

14. Internet Access

EFFROSYNI is designed to operate locally on your device.

The Android application is not designed to send your workload or personal app data over the Internet.

15. Data Retention

Your EFFROSYNI data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the application, clear the application data through your operating system, or uninstall the application.

The developer does not retain a server-side copy of your EFFROSYNI app data.

16. Data Deletion

Because EFFROSYNI does not require an account and does not store your app data on developer-controlled servers, there is no remote account or cloud profile to delete.

You can delete your local EFFROSYNI data by using the application's reset functionality, where available, by clearing the app's data through your device settings, or by uninstalling the application.

Deleting the application does not automatically delete backup or export files that you previously chose to save elsewhere.

Those files must be deleted separately from the location where you saved them.

17. Security

EFFROSYNI uses technical measures designed to protect locally stored information, including encrypted local storage on supported Android installations and application access controls.

However, no device or software system can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, operating system, device lock, PINs, passwords, and exported backup files.

18. Third-Party Services

EFFROSYNI does not rely on third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services to operate its core functionality.

If you choose to save an exported file to a third-party storage provider, that provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to its handling of that file.

EFFROSYNI does not control third-party services selected by you.

19. Medical and Professional Disclaimer

EFFROSYNI is a personal workload and scheduling intelligence tool.

It is not a medical device.

It does not provide medical, psychological, diagnostic, therapeutic, occupational health, legal, accounting, or financial advice.

Information displayed by EFFROSYNI should not be used as a substitute for professional judgment or professional advice.

20. Children's Privacy

EFFROSYNI is intended for adult professionals.

It is not designed for use by children.

The application is not intended to collect personal information from children.

21. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application changes, if new functionality is introduced, or if legal or platform requirements change.

When the Privacy Policy is updated, the Effective Date will also be updated.

Material changes may also be communicated through the application or its official distribution page where appropriate.
